Kampala Plate Site Plan

A planning document for a local food discovery website.

Site Name

Kampala Plate

This name was chosen because the website will introduce visitors to memorable meals, restaurants, and local food experiences in Kampala. The word "plate" connects directly to food while keeping the name simple and easy to remember.

Optional domain: kampalaplate.org

Site Purpose

Kampala Plate will provide a helpful guide to popular dishes, recommended restaurants, market food spots, and practical tips for people who want to explore food in Kampala. The site will include food categories, location details, price guidance, and a contact form for visitors who want recommendations.

Scenarios

  • Where can I find affordable local meals near central Kampala?
  • Which Ugandan dishes should a first-time visitor try?
  • How can I contact the guide for a restaurant suggestion?

Typography

Merriweather will be used for the page title and main section headings because it feels established and editorial.

Nunito will be used for body text, lists, cards, and navigation because it is clean, readable, and friendly.
